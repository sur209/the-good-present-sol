## Current contract

- Source of truth: stable-ID-named JSON files under `content/`.
- Shared boundary: pure schemas, validation, error formatting, and route helpers in `packages/content-schema/`.
- Public output: static Astro assets in `apps/site/dist/`.
- Merchant destinations: validated affiliate-first resolution from the central product catalog; no internal outbound redirect exists.
- Affiliate operations: non-public records under `editorial-data/affiliate-programs/` and a read-only Studio status page; no secrets or automatic link generation.
- Product provenance: non-public records under `editorial-data/product-sources/`; canonical products remain independent editorial entities, and source identifiers never enter Astro output.
- Product discovery: `/product-sourcing` reuses the I.2 request/candidate lifecycle and current editorial provider for bounded SearchPlans; exactly one explicitly configured external source may add observed evidence, while DataForSEO defaults off and no source may create, fulfill, assign, or publish a Product automatically.
- Product intelligence: `/product-intelligence` computes a read-only Studio report from canonical records, GuideDrafts, and structured product-gap reports; its thresholds and evidence remain non-public and never authorize or create editorial work.
- Recommendation compatibility: existing Product-backed public records remain unchanged; a ready idea may publish only with `productResolution: "unresolved"`, no `productId`, and no Product, merchant, price, or shopping UI. Its stable recommendation ID and I.0 gap survive later Product resolution or replacement.
- Content Opportunity Lab: `/opportunities` reads validated candidates from `editorial-data/article-candidates/`, compares them locally with public content, drafts, approved briefs, and prior decisions, and can run intent-first, product-first, or coverage-first divergent ideation plus convergent evaluation through the existing provider. Generated candidates receive system identity, session mode, exact source provenance, and 5.1 comparison; selected batches receive authoritative 5.1 and I.0 evidence plus optional provenance-dated imports. AI can advance only to `evaluated`. Human approval may create an internal brief under `editorial-data/editorial-briefs/`; a separately approved brief may create one ordinary Goal 2 GuideDraft, while products, canonical content, publication, and public routes remain unchanged.
- Manual product intake: `/products/intake` creates one canonical product plus one linked source record only after explicit preview confirmation; source facts, ASINs, affiliate provenance, and image-rights notes remain non-public.
- Amazon intake: manually generated SiteStripe/Associates Central links only; local validation is string-based and writes the public affiliate URL only after confirmation, with original URL and ASIN provenance kept in the non-public source ledger.
- URL policy: only differentiated, substantial editorial intents receive routes; taxonomies never create pages.
- MVP scope: Nurse Gifts hub plus graduation, practical, and under-$25 guides.
